## Task

Your answer must be the factual entity, name, number, or date that is correct
UNDER THAT SPECIFIC ASSUMPTION — not the most famous or default answer.

## Rules
1. Your answer MUST be ≤ 5 words. Output ONLY the answer entity.
2. STRICTLY follow the assumption. If the assumption specifies a particular year, TV series,
   edition, or context, answer for THAT specific version — not the most well-known one.

4. If the assumption points to an obscure but real entity, output that entity.
   DO NOT fall back to the most famous answer.
5. If Assumptions say "None.", answer the question with its most common interpretation.
